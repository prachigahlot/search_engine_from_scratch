# Evidence Policy

Deep distinguishes six evidence states.

## FACT

Supported by documentation, source material, or another explicit reliable source.

## MEASUREMENT

Observed directly in an experiment.

## INFERENCE

An explanation derived from facts and/or measurements.

## HYPOTHESIS

A proposition that could be tested and potentially falsified.

## PROPOSAL

A suggested design or future change that has not yet been established by evidence.

## UNKNOWN

Something the available evidence does not establish.

## Rule

Never convert an inference into a fact merely because it sounds plausible.

Never invent a measurement.

Never present a reconstruction as Google's internal implementation unless public evidence establishes it.

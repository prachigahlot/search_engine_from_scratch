# Google Public Evidence

## Purpose

Use Google as a deep public case-study lens without pretending to know undocumented internal implementation details.

For every source, record:

### Source

Title, publisher, date, and link.

### Explicitly documented

What the source actually states.

### Reasonable inference

What can be inferred from the documented material.

### Our reconstruction

What mechanism we implement as an engineering model or experiment.

### Unknown

What the public evidence does not establish.

## Rule

Do not write “Google does X internally” unless the cited public evidence actually establishes X.
